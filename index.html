Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to English பேசலாம் ("we," "our," or "us"), developed by Alaga Software Tech. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your privacy rights. This Privacy Policy details what information we collect, how it is processed, how it is protected, and your choices regarding data retention and account deletion.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect data strictly necessary to facilitate your language learning journey and ensure account synchronization:

  • Authentication Information: When you register or sign in via Google Sign-In or Mobile Phone Authentication (SMS OTP), we receive standard identity data including your name, email address, profile picture (if provided via Google), or mobile phone number.
  • Learning Progress & Activity Data: Metrics such as completed lessons, daily learning streak counters, XP points, and bookmarked/saved vocabulary items. This information is saved to maintain consistency across app sessions and devices.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Diagnostic logs and standard crash information provided via Firebase services solely for error troubleshooting and stability enhancements.

2. How We Use and Share Your Information

  • To verify identity, establish your learner profile, and synchronize educational progress via secure cloud databases.
  • To diagnose application bugs, maintain infrastructure uptime, and deliver system improvements.
  • No Data Brokering: We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal information to third parties, advertising brokers, or marketing networks. Data is only shared with backend infrastructure partners (Google Firebase) required to operate the application.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure Services

Our application interfaces with trusted Google Cloud developer platforms:

  • Firebase Authentication: Manages secure credential verification via Google Accounts and Phone SMS OTP.
  • Cloud Firestore: Provides secure cloud database storage for learning metrics and user records.
  • Google Play Services: Supports core operating system features, updates, and platform dependencies.

4. Data Security

We enforce industry-standard security safeguards. All communications between the mobile application and cloud backend databases are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS). Access to database records is restricted through defined Firebase security rules ensuring users can only read and write their own profile records.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you discover that a child under 13 has submitted personal information without verified guardian consent, contact us immediately to facilitate prompt deletion of those records.

6. Account and Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

Users have full control over their account data and may request permanent deletion at any time via two pathways:

In-App Deletion: Open the app > Navigate to Profile (சுயவிவரம்) > Tap Delete Account (கணக்கை நீக்குக). Your authentication record, learning milestones, and saved vocabulary will be immediately purged.

Web / Email Deletion Request: If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, send an email to alagarmca006@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request - English Pesalam" from your registered email or providing your registered phone number. All associated personal records will be verified and permanently deleted within 7 business days.
